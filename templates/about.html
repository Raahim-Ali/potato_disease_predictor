About the Potato Disease Predictor

The Potato Disease Predictor is an intelligent web application built to empower farmers and agricultural experts by predicting potential potato crop diseases using real-time weather data and machine learning.

By analyzing key indicators such as temperature, humidity, and seasonal patterns, this tool provides early alerts and actionable insights. This enables timely interventions, leading to healthier crops, reduced losses, and improved yield.

Our goal is to bridge the gap between AI technology and everyday farming practices, making data-driven decisions accessible, intuitive, and impactful for everyone in agriculture.
